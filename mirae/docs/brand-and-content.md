# Brand & Content

## Voice
| Is | Isn't |
|---|---|
| Calm, precise, quietly confident: "Here's what's in it, and what heat does to it." | Hype ("miracle", "glow overnight"), fear-based, influencer slang |
| Transparent: shows lab numbers, including the unflattering ones | Vague "clinically proven" without a citation |
| Ritual-minded: the morning cup as self-care | Medical: no disease, no drug comparisons |
| Korean-inspired in sensibility (layered routines, *jeong*, care) | Appropriative: no fake Korean origin, no K-pop name-dropping |

**Tagline:** *The future of your morning.* · **Sign-off:** *One cup. Your whole routine.*

## Colour & type direction
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Peach Glow | `#F4B6A0` | Primary (box, hero) |
| Rose Copper | `#B0654A` | Wordmark, headings |
| Rice Paper | `#FBF6F1` | Backgrounds |
| Espresso | `#3A2620` | Body text |
| Celadon (accent) | `#9DB8A6` | "Tested" badges, links |

- **Type:**
  - Wordmark and display: a high-contrast serif (e.g. *Cormorant Garamond*).
  - Body: *Pretendard* or *Noto Sans KR*. Both are open-licence and cover Hangul, so you can add a "미래" mark.
- **Imagery:** soft daylight, marble and linen, steam, one product per frame, real hands. No stock-model skin close-ups implying results.

## Six content pillars
| Pillar | Share | Example formats |
|---|---|---|
| Education | 25% | "What 250 mg of vitamin C is", "Collagen peptides vs gelatin" |
| Transparency | 20% | Lab report walkthroughs, "what heat does to vitamin C, in numbers", cost breakdown of a box |
| Routine | 20% | 30-second morning ritual, desk setup, "habit stacking" |
| Science | 10% | Plain-language study summaries with citations (no efficacy promises) |
| Behind-the-scenes | 15% | Founder building in Queens, supplier calls, label drafts, mistakes |
| Social proof | 10% | Taste-test reactions, customer routines (only real ones, never paid testimonials without disclosure) |
