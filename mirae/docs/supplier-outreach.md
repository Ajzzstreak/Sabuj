# Supplier Outreach

**Assumption:** suppliers are private-label or white-label manufacturers that **already sell** a vitamin and/or collagen instant coffee. MIRAE only changes the label (and, where offered, the flavor or dose within their validated range). I found no published MOQ or price data for collagen-coffee stick packs, so every figure must come from quotes.

## Shortlist template
| Supplier | Country | Existing formula (yes/no + name) | Format (stick/jar) | Collagen per serving | Vit C per serving (label / post-brew) | MOQ | Unit price at MOQ | Setup / plate fees | Lead time | COA | cGMP cert / FDA food facility reg. | Score /100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | | | | | | |

**Where to find candidates:**
- B2B directories (Wonnda, Alibaba "trade assurance", ThomasNet). Filter for "instant coffee" plus "stick pack" or "sachet", and "collagen".
- US co-packers that run powder stick-pack lines.
- Korean ODMs. These suit the brand story, but **add import/FDA prior-notice complexity**.

## Email script
> **Subject:** Private-label inquiry: collagen + vitamin C instant coffee stick packs (300–500 units)
>
> Hello [Name],
>
> I'm launching MIRAE, a US wellness coffee brand. I'd like to private-label an **existing** formula from you rather than develop a new one. Could you please send:
>
> 1. Your current vitamin/collagen instant coffee formulas (spec sheets), including per-serving amounts of collagen, vitamin C, biotin and caffeine
> 2. **MOQ** per SKU, and **unit pricing** at 300, 500 and 1,000 boxes (20 sticks/box), including printed stick film and retail box
> 3. A recent **Certificate of Analysis** for a production lot (potency, heavy metals, micro)
> 4. Proof of **cGMP** compliance and **FDA food facility registration** number
> 5. **Post-brew vitamin C data**: measured vitamin C after preparing one stick in ~90 °C water as directed, with method and lab name
> 6. How you manage heat losses: **overage %** and/or **encapsulation** (coated ascorbate etc.)
> 7. Whether the product is labeled as a **conventional food (Nutrition Facts)** or a **dietary supplement (Supplement Facts)** in the US, and why
> 8. **Sample terms** (cost, quantity, shipping time) and production **lead time** and **payment terms** (deposit %, net terms on reorders)
> 9. Shelf life and the stability data behind it
>
> I can move quickly on samples this month. Thank you,
> [Name], Founder, MIRAE · [email] · drinkmirae.com (placeholder)

**Automatic walk-away:**
- The supplier claims "100% vitamin delivery after brewing".
- The supplier can't provide a COA or facility registration.
- The supplier refuses to say how the product is classified.

## Scoring rubric (100 points)
| Criterion | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| Post-brew data quality | 20 | Third-party lab data, overage/encapsulation explained, realistic loss stated |
| Compliance evidence | 20 | cGMP certificate, FDA registration, clean recent COA (heavy metals, micro) |
| Unit economics | 20 | Landed ≤ $5.00/box at ≤ 500 MOQ (≤ $6.00 = 10 pts) |
| MOQ fit | 10 | ≤ 300 boxes (≤ 500 = 5 pts) |
| Formula fit | 10 | Collagen ≥ 5 g/serving, vitamin C present, biotin at or near 100% DV rather than mega-dose |
| Taste (blind panel) | 10 | ≥ 7/10 average |
| Lead time & terms | 5 | ≤ 6 weeks; net terms on reorders |
| Responsiveness | 5 | Complete answers within 5 business days |

**Decision rule:** pick the highest score ≥ 75. If two are within 5 points, choose the lower MOQ.
