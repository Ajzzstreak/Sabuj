# 90-Day Launch Plan (≤ $5,000)

**Assumptions:**
- 300-box first PO at $5.00/box with a 50% deposit.
- Pre-order target of 60 boxes at $24.99.
- Self-fulfilment from home. 300 boxes at an assumed 6×3.5×2.5 in ≈ **9 cu ft**, about one closet shelf, so "no storage" is not a blocker at this volume.
- 3PL only once orders reach ≥200/month, because 3PL monthly minimums are about $250–275.

All amounts mirror `model/financial_model.xlsx → Launch Budget` (formulas). Cash numbers below are recalculated outputs.

## Validation gate (end of week 6)
**GO only if every hard criterion passes:**

| # | Criterion | Threshold | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Waitlist sign-ups | ≥ 300 emails | Enough audience to convert into 60 pre-orders |
| 2 | Paid validation efficiency | ≤ $2.00 per waitlist sign-up on the $300 test | Signals a CAC under $15 is reachable |
| 3 | Supplier | ≥ 1 supplier scoring ≥ 75/100, with a COA, cGMP evidence and **post-brew vitamin C data** | Quality gate |
| 4 | Unit cost | Landed ≤ $6.00/box at ≤ 500 MOQ | Keeps contribution ≥ $11 |
| 5 | Lab result | Vitamin C after brewing ≥ 90% of the label claim (or the claim is lowered to match) | Label honesty |
| 6 | Taste | ≥ 7/10 average from ≥ 8 target customers | RTD and functional products fail on taste |

**Second gate (end of week 7, before the PO):** ≥ **60 paid pre-orders**, or ≥ 15% of the waitlist converting.
- If you get 30–59: order the supplier's minimum only, if it's ≤ 300.
- If you get under 30: **refund everyone and do not order inventory.**

Pre-orders must ship within the stated time or within 30 days, or customers must be offered a refund (FTC Mail Order Rule). State "ships in ~5 weeks" on the page.

## After day 90: the cash constraint
The 12-month model (`CF Realistic`) shows cash going **negative from month 2 to month 12, bottoming at −$3,837**, if growth follows plan pace with $15 CAC. That's because inventory reorders and paid acquisition run ahead of subscription revenue. Three ways to handle it, ranked:
1. **Throttle paid ads so month-end cash stays ≥ $500.** Grow on organic content plus subscriptions, and accept slower growth.
2. **Add up to $5K (your $10K ceiling)** only after month-2 data shows subscription retention ≥ 85%/month.
3. Ask the supplier for net-30 terms on reorders. This moves the deposit dip later.
