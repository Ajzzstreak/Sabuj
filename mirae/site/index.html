Questions

Does hot water destroy the vitamin C?

Some vitamin C breaks down with heat and time. That's why we measure the brewed cup, not just the powder, and publish the result. For best results, stir into hot (not boiling) water and enjoy within a few minutes.

Is there caffeine?

Yes, it's real coffee. The exact amount per stick will be printed on the box.

What kind of collagen?

Hydrolyzed collagen peptides. The source (bovine or marine) will be confirmed on the label before pre-orders open, along with any allergens.
