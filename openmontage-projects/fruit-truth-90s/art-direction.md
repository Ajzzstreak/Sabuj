# Art Direction — "Good or Bad? 6 Fruits in 90 Seconds"

**Design read:** Market-stall lab. Flat vector fruit, chunky soft shadows, every fruit on its own saturated
color field. Feels like a produce label crossed with a lab readout.

**Taste dials:** visual_variance 0.7 · motion_intensity 0.75 · information_density 0.55

**Palette (per scene field):** hook ink #17162B · banana teal #0F5257 · apple peach #FFE3CF ·
avocado cocoa #3A271C · blueberry pink #FFC6DA · orange cobalt #1D3B8F · mango plum #5E2350 · outro ink.
Verdict colors: good #2BB673, portion amber #F2A33A, watch-out #E5484D.

**Type:** Space Grotesk (heavy for labels / huge ghost fruit names), Playfair italic for "or".

**Signature device:** the deconstruction — each fruit opens in its own way (banana peels, apple
splits to show the star core, avocado halves & the pit pops, blueberries scatter and one is sliced,
orange segments fan out, mango hedgehog cubes pop).

**Anti-patterns:** no stock cut types, no generic stat cards, no identical entrance per fruit.
